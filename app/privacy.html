Privacy Policy

Collage Studio · Last updated September 28, 2026

Short version: Collage Studio doesn't collect, send, or sell any of your information. Your photos and collages stay on your own computer.

Your photos and collages

Everything happens on your device. Photos you add are read by the app on your computer and are never uploaded. Collages are saved only where you choose to save them. PDF, JPEG and printed copies go only where you send them.

What is stored on your device

To protect your work if the app or your computer closes unexpectedly, Collage Studio keeps a recovery copy of the collage you're working on (including its photos), plus a short list of recently opened collages. This is kept in the app's private storage on your computer and never leaves it. Discarding the recovery copy or clearing the app's data removes it.

No accounts, tracking, or ads

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies, and no third-party services inside the app. After it's installed, the app works offline.

Hosting

The app's files are delivered by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when the app is downloaded or updated. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Collage Studio doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions are welcome through GitHub Issues.
