Collage Studio help

Open Collage Studio

Making a collage

  1. Click New collage, pick a page size (US Letter is already chosen) and a layout.
  2. Click Add photos, or drag pictures from File Explorer onto the page. They fill the empty slots.
  3. Drag one photo onto another to swap them. Double-click a photo to crop it.
  4. Use the panel on the right to rotate, adjust colors, change the frame shape, or switch a slot to text.
  5. Click Add text for a title or caption you can put anywhere, even on top of a photo.
  6. Feeling lucky? Click Surprise me for a random layout of the photos on the page. Click it again for another one, and choose Scattered in the side panel for tilted, overlapping prints.
  7. Click Save to keep your collage so you can change it later.
  8. Click Print, Save PDF or Save JPEG.

Common questions

How do I save a PDF?

Click Save PDF. In the print window, set the printer (or destination) to Save as PDF and click Save. Choose “Save as PDF” rather than “Microsoft Print to PDF”, because it keeps your exact page size.

My print has white edges or is the wrong size

In the print window, make sure Scale is 100% (or “Actual size”), and that the paper size matches your collage. Most home printers can't print all the way to the edge, so a thin white border is normal. Photo labs print edge to edge from a PDF or JPEG.

My iPhone photos won't add

iPhones save photos as HEIC files, which Collage Studio can't read yet. On the iPhone, open Settings › Photos and under “Transfer to Mac or PC” choose Automatic. Photos copied to your computer will then be JPEGs. You can also convert HEIC files with the Windows Photos app (Save as JPEG).

Where are my collages saved?

Wherever you choose when you click Save. Collage files end in .collage and contain copies of your photos, so they still work if you move or delete the originals. Double-click a .collage file to open it.

The app closed before I saved

Open Collage Studio again and click Pick up where I left off.

Does it need the internet?

No. Once it's installed it works offline, and your photos are never uploaded.

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+O open · Ctrl+P print · Delete remove the selected item · Enter crop a photo or edit text · Esc cancel · arrow keys move between slots or nudge text.
